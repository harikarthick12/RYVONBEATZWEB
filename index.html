Questions? Answers.

  • Ryvon Music (RYVON BEATZ) is an independent, premium Android music player designed around a clean and immersive listening experience. It features bit-perfect sound fidelity, synchronized lyrics, dynamic album color themes, and complete freedom from commercial ads or tracking.

  • Ryvon Music is distributed as an Android APK compatible with Android 8.0 Oreo through Android 15+. You can listen on Android smartphones, tablets, foldables, digital audio players (DAPs), Android Auto, and via Windows Subsystem for Android (WSA) or desktop emulators.

  • Ryvon Music is completely free. There are no monthly subscription fees, in-app purchases, or locked features. All audio capabilities—including synchronized lyrics, bit-perfect DAC output, and the hardware DSP equalizer—are available to everyone at ₹0.

  • Spatial Audio uses advanced psychoacoustic filtering and 3D soundstage virtualization to simulate surround sound through any standard headphones or earbuds. It widens the stereo field and creates the sensation that music is surrounding you in a live concert hall.

  • Lossless audio preserves 100% of the acoustic data from original studio master recordings. While standard Android audio resamples all signals to 48kHz, Ryvon Music provides direct USB Audio Class 2.0 bypass to output native 24-bit/192kHz to external DACs and Hi-Fi equipment without degradation.

  • Zero. Ryvon Music is strictly ad-free. No banner ads, interstitial video ads, audio sponsorships, or analytics trackers are bundled with the software.

  • Yes. The entire codebase is licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0) and maintained publicly on GitHub. Anyone is welcome to inspect, audit, or contribute to development.
