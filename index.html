QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the RYVON BEATZ Android music player.

What is RYVON BEATZ?

RYVON BEATZ is an independent, premium Android music player designed around a clean and immersive listening experience. It features bit-perfect sound fidelity, synchronized lyrics, dynamic album color themes, and complete freedom from commercial ads or tracking.

Is RYVON BEATZ available for Android?

Yes. RYVON BEATZ is built natively for Android (supporting Android 8.0 Oreo through Android 14+) and is distributed as a clean, self-contained Android APK directly through the official RYVON BEATZ website and GitHub Releases.

How do I install RYVON BEATZ APK?

Download either the Universal APK or the ARM64 optimized APK from our official website, tap the downloaded file in your notification shade or file manager, and confirm installation when prompted by Android ("Allow from this source"). You can consult our Installation Guide for full step-by-step instructions.

Does RYVON BEATZ support synchronized lyrics?

Yes. RYVON BEATZ features real-time, line-by-line synchronized typography aligned smoothly with your audio playback, complete with kinetic auto-scrolling that keeps every verse in rhythm.

Can I play offline music and FLAC lossless files?

Absolutely. RYVON BEATZ features lightning-fast local storage indexing with comprehensive support for lossless formats including FLAC (up to 24-bit/192kHz), WAV, ALAC, AAC, and MP3 with true gapless playback.

Is RYVON BEATZ free of ads and subscriptions?

Yes. RYVON BEATZ is 100% free and open source. There are zero audio ads, banner promotions, tracking beacons, analytics harvest, or paywalls. Your listening data never leaves your device.

Is RYVON BEATZ affiliated with YouTube or Google?

No. RYVON BEATZ is an independent third-party open-source project and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Google LLC or YouTube.

Is RYVON BEATZ open source?

Yes. The entire codebase is licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0). You are welcome to inspect, audit, fork, and contribute directly via our GitHub repository.
